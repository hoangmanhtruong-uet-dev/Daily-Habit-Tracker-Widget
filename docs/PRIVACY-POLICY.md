# Privacy Policy for HabitGrid

*Last updated: October 21, 2026*

HabitGrid ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding your information.

## 1. Information Collection and Storage
- **No Personal Data Collection**: HabitGrid does NOT collect, transmit, share, or store any personal information, telemetry, usage statistics, or habit tracking records on external servers.
- **100% Local Storage**: All your habits, streaks, settings, and daily entries are stored exclusively on your local computer in a SQLite database located at `%LocalAppData%\HabitGrid\habitgrid.db`.
- **No Cloud Accounts**: You do not need to create an account, log in, or provide an email address to use the application.

## 2. Network Usage & Third-Party Services
- The only network requests made by HabitGrid are directly to the **Microsoft Store APIs** (`Windows.Services.Store`) to:
  - Verify your license status (Free vs. Pro).
  - Process optional in-app purchases securely via your Microsoft account.
- We do not have access to your payment methods, credit card details, or billing addresses. All financial transactions are handled exclusively by Microsoft Corporation.

## 3. Data Backup and Export
- When you use the export feature (JSON or CSV), files are saved directly to the folder you specify on your local device. We have no access to these exported files.

## 4. Children's Privacy
- HabitGrid does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

## 5. Contact Information
If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact developer support via GitHub repository issues or developer contact email.
